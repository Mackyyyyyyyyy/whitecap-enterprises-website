Built for Draper's hillside terrain and tech-corridor growth

Draper was settled in 1849-1850 by Ebenezer and Phebe Draper Brown, and spent much of the next century as farmland, earning the nickname "Egg Basket of Utah" in the 1940s for its poultry industry. That's a long way from today's Draper, which has grown from about 7,100 residents in 1990 to over 51,000 by 2020 and now hosts corporate headquarters for companies like 1-800 Contacts, Control4, HealthEquity, Pluralsight, and an eBay campus along the Silicon Slopes tech corridor.

Sitting in the far southeast corner of the Salt Lake Valley against the Wasatch Range at around 4,750 feet, Draper's terrain runs from flat valley-floor lots to steep hillside neighborhoods like Corner Canyon and Suncrest. Hillside homes see more wind exposure and steeper rooflines, both of which put extra demand on attic insulation and garage door seals that a flat-lot house in the valley doesn't have to deal with.

We handle insulation and garage door work across both Draper's valley-floor neighborhoods and its hillside communities.
