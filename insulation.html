FAQ

Insulation FAQ

What types of insulation do you install?
We install spray foam, attic, fiberglass, blown-in, and soundproofing insulation. Each has its own strengths, and we recommend the right material based on the space, your goals, and your budget.
Which insulation is best for my home?
It depends on where it is going and what you want to achieve, whether that is lower energy bills, better comfort, moisture control, or sound reduction. During a free assessment we look at your property and recommend the best fit.
What are the benefits of upgrading my insulation?
Better insulation lowers heating and cooling bills, keeps indoor temperatures more consistent, reduces drafts, and can help with moisture and noise. In Utah's climate, it is one of the most cost-effective home upgrades available.
How much does insulation cost?
Cost depends on the size of the area, the type of insulation, and the condition of what is already there. That is why we always provide a free, no-obligation estimate before any work starts.
Do you offer free insulation estimates?
Yes. Call (385) 541-2333 or request a quote online for a free, no-pressure estimate anywhere in our Northern and Central Utah service area.
