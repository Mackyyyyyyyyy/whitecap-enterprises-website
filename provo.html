Insulation for Provo's historic homes, rentals, and new builds

Founded in 1849 and incorporated the following year, Provo has grown into Utah County's largest city, with a 2020 population over 115,000, up from roughly 87,000 just three decades earlier. Brigham Young University, the largest private religious university in the country with more than 30,000 students, sits at the heart of the city and shapes its housing market in a way few other Utah cities experience: dense pockets of student rental housing sit alongside long-established family neighborhoods and newer subdivisions on the city's edges.

That mix means Provo has some of the oldest housing stock in Utah County, dating back to the city's early-1900s "Garden City" era and its 1920s industrial expansion, right alongside modern construction. Older owner-occupied and rental homes alike often have outdated or settled insulation, while rental properties in particular can go years without anyone checking on attic coverage or garage door condition.

We work across Provo's older established neighborhoods, its student rental housing, and its newer developments.
