Services FAQ

What services does Whitecap Enterprises offer?
We provide two core services: insulation (spray foam, attic, fiberglass, blown-in, and soundproofing) and garage doors (installation, replacement, and repair). Having both trades under one roof means a single, accountable contractor for your project.
Do you work on both homes and businesses?
Yes. We serve homeowners, landlords, and commercial property owners across Northern and Central Utah, from single-family homes and rentals to shops, sheds, and light commercial buildings.
What areas do you serve?
We are based in Pleasant Grove and work throughout Utah County and the greater Salt Lake area, including Lehi, American Fork, Provo, Saratoga Springs, Draper, South Jordan, Salt Lake City, and more.
Are you licensed and insured?
Yes. Whitecap Enterprises LLC is a fully licensed and insured contractor, so your property and our crew are protected on every job.
How do I get a quote?
Call (385) 541-2333 or request a free quote online. We will talk through your project and provide a clear, no-pressure estimate.

Not sure which service you need?

Tell us what's going on and we'll point you in the right direction, no charge for the estimate.
